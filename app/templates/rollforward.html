Copy a whole year's trials to the next year in one step. Dates shift to the same weekend and day of the week (a Saturday–Sunday trial stays a Saturday–Sunday trial). Club, location, and stakes carry over; judges, fees, closing dates, links and notes do not — those change every year, so the new events start blank and each one can be opened to fill them in or adjust its date. You will see every proposed event before anything is created, and a mistaken run can be undone as a batch.
